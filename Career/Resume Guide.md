# Resume Gap Guide

Here is how you can explicitly address each of these missing pieces on your resume.

To bridge these gaps effectively, you should translate your implicit background into explicit, high-impact bullet points using quantifiable metrics.

Below is a breakdown of how to frame each requirement, along with plug-and-play bullet point examples you can adapt.

---

## 1. Team Leadership & People Management

| Core Skill / Gap | How to Address It | Example Bullet Point |
|---|---|---|
| **Lead 4–7 engineers** | State the exact size of the team and your structural role. | Directly managed and led a high-performing team of 6 software engineers, aligning sprint deliverables with product roadmaps to ensure 95%+ on-time feature delivery. |
| **Coaching & career development** | Frame this as active professional growth and role progression. | Coached and mentored 4 mid-level engineers, facilitating their career progression and successfully promoting 2 into senior engineering tracks. |
| **Hiring & onboarding** | Highlight building the team and reducing the time-to-productivity for new hires. | Scales engineering capacity by owning the hiring lifecycle, interviewing 30+ candidates, onboarding 3 new engineers, and reducing ramp-up time by 25%. |
| **Performance management** | Quantify evaluations, constructive feedback, and alignment. | Managed quarterly and annual performance review cycles, establishing OKRs and KPI frameworks that boosted overall team velocity by 18%. |
| **Staff/Principal dev** | Highlight your role in guiding senior technical leaders, not just juniors. | Guided technical growth plans for 2 Staff-track engineers, providing strategic oversight on complex system architecture and cross-team alignment. |

## 2. Advanced Technical Domains

### Agentic AI

If you don't have direct experience building autonomous agents, look for adjacent workloads where you built intelligent automation, event-driven workflows, or integrated LLMs.

- **Example:** Architected intelligent event-driven workflows leveraging generative AI APIs to automate internal decision-making processes, reducing manual verification time by 40%.

### Document Intelligence

Tie this to parsing, OCR, data extraction, or processing large unstructured data streams.

- **Example:** Designed and implemented a document intelligence pipeline utilizing OCR and NLP technologies to automate the extraction and validation of unstructured PDF data at enterprise scale.

### TypeScript

If you have used JavaScript, Node.js, or modern frontend frameworks, explicit mention of TypeScript is essential for modern Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS).

- **Example:** Led the migration of a legacy Node.js backend to TypeScript, reducing runtime errors by 30% and vastly improving codebase maintainability and developer ergonomics.

---

## 3. Strategy & Evolution

### Startup → Enterprise Transition

Emphasize the evolution of engineering practices—such as scaling a loose process into a rigorous, compliant architecture.

- **Example:** Spearheaded the engineering transition from startup speed to enterprise-grade compliance, introducing formalized change management and data governance frameworks to support 10x user growth.

### Organizational Integration / Acquisition

If you haven't been acquired, highlight cross-functional system integrations or consolidating fragmented platforms into a single architecture.

- **Example:** Orchestrated the technical integration of two disparate platform architectures, consolidating shared identity services and eliminating $150K in redundant annual infrastructure overhead.

---

## 4. Operations & Infrastructure

### Reliability / Observability (Operational Ownership)

Pivot away from just mentioning tools (like Splunk or Kubernetes). Frame it as taking ultimate accountability for system health, service level objectives (SLOs), and incident response.

- **Example:** Established full operational ownership of cloud infrastructure, defining core SLIs/SLOs and configuring advanced Splunk/Kubernetes alerting to reduce Mean Time to Resolution (MTTR) by 45%.
