### How to Use These Resources Effectively

To avoid feeling overwhelmed by the sheer volume of material, follow these guidelines:

1️⃣ Prioritize Primary Resources
> Ensure you’ve thoroughly mastered the essential resources before moving on to additional material.

2️⃣ Gap Analysis
> Identify weaknesses in your understanding after covering the primary resources. Use complementary material to bridge these gaps.

3️⃣ Iterative Learning
> Treat your preparation as an iterative process.

4️⃣ Time Management
> Be selective about what you consume. Don’t feel compelled to cover everything. Focus on resources that provide the best return on your investment of time.
