Welcome to Osborne Interactive Tools & Training. On this CD-Rom there is a 
mixture of products, each product has built-in help files if needed.  

This CD-ROM has an auto play feature. If the auto play feature did not launch your CD, 
open the CD and Double-click on LaunchTraining.exe. This will open the Interactive Tools & 
Training CD.

SETUP INSTRUCTIONS:

Step 1: Insert the CD-Rom to initiate the auto play feature
Step 2:  On the Entry Screen, you will be able to launch various Tools and Training. Just 
click the area that would most interest you. You may choose from MasterExam, Bonus
MasterExam, & the Electronic Book.  


ENTRY SCREEN LINKS:

The MasterExam is a testing environment that will provide you with a wide variety of 
tasks to prepare you for the Certification test.  Once you have installed the MasterExam 
you may access it quickly through this CD launch page or by completing the following 
steps: 

1. Click the Start button
2. Point to Programs
3. Point to LearnKey
4. Point to MasterExam
5. Click on LearnKey MasterExam

The Bonus MasterExam is a second free downloadable MasterExam.  In order to access
and install the Bonus MasterExam, complete the following steps:

1. Click the Bonus MasterExam link on the main page.
2. Click Register Now
3. Select the course that matches the Study Guide
4. Fill out the form completely and click the Proceed button
5. Click on Student Login, use your email address & the password that was emailed to you.
6. Click the course name
7. Click the MasterExam Tab at the top of the page
8. Click the link to download the MasterExam
9. Once the download is complete, double-click on the dowloaded file to launch setup.

Once you have installed the Bonus MasterExam, you can quickly access by following the
directions given in the MasterExam section above.


REMOVING INSTALLATION(S):

The MasterExam is installed to your hard drive.  For BEST results for removal of the 
program complete the following steps:

1. Click the Start button
2. Point to Programs
3. Point to LearnKey
4. Point to Uninstall
5. Click on LearnKey MasterExam uninstall.
