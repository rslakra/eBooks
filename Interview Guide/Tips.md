Tips for Early, Mid, and Senior-Level Candidates

Early Career (1–3 Years of Experience)
----------------------------------------

Focus: Building technical expertise, quick learning, and producing high-quality work with guidance.

Interview:
• Solve coding challenges focused on algorithms, data structures, and basic design.
• Show you can take hints and write clean, readable code.
• Expectations increase with experience - be ready to explain your thought process.

Mid-Level (3–6 Years of Experience)
----------------------------------------

Focus: Independent implementation, ownership of problem-solving, and supervised design.

Interview (in addition to Early Career):
• Solve problems with multiple solutions, analyzing trade-offs (e.g., complexity, scalability).
• Avoid assumptions - explore alternatives thoroughly.
• Discuss real-world experience in areas like concurrency, testing, and robust design.
• Clean, maintainable code with minimal hints is key.

Senior-Level (6+ Years of Experience)
----------------------------------------

Focus: Leading multi-quarter projects, independently scoping problems, and proposing well-researched solutions.

Interview (in addition to Mid-Level):
• Analyze trade-offs in depth and question problem definitions where necessary.
• Deliver clean, well-tested, and well-designed code.
• Strong emphasis on API design, error handling, scalability, and testability.
• Design interviews are critical: Show system-level thinking and the ability to integrate solutions across teams.

Key takeaways:
----------------------------------------
• Master coding fundamentals and algorithms.
• Understand and articulate design principles.
• Be prepared to discuss past experience and challenges.
• Highlight problem-solving and collaboration skills.
• For senior roles, system design expertise and leadership
